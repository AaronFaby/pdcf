      CONTROLLERS: Stick / D-pad directions stay fixed; action buttons can be remapped in Options → Controls. Current bindings appear below the arena. Start always pauses. In menus A confirms and B goes back. Release buttons after changing screens.

      TRAINING: Select Training before starting. Choose Learn to fight for eight guided lessons using your saved bindings, or practice freely with combat feedback.
