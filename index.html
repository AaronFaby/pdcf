A FREE DESKTOP BROWSER FIGHTING GAME

Public Domain Cartoon Fighters

Step into an independent 2D fighting game inspired by 1930s and 1940s rubber-hose animation. Play instantly in your desktop browser with Mickey, Minnie, Oswald, Felix, Betty and Popeye, adapted from the early character versions described in our character notices. No account or installation is required.

Choose your bout

Face the CPU at Easy, Normal or Hard difficulty. Share a keyboard or use standard controllers for local two-player matches. Arcade takes your fighter through a six-bout ladder to an original ending. Training offers guided lessons, practice options, move lists and combat feedback.

Fight on Ink Harbor, Silverscreen Theater, Moonlight Midway or Midnight Junction. Each stage has animated scenery and an original period-inspired score.

How to play

Choose a mode, both fighters and a stage, then select “Let’s make a scene.” Win two rounds by knocking out your rival or finishing the round with more health. Move, jump, guard, punch, kick, throw and spend ink on special moves.

Use How to play in the top menu for instructions. Open Options → Controls & player assignments for your current keyboard and controller bindings. Training’s “Learn to fight” lessons teach the basics using those bindings.

This is a desktop beta with provisional balance and limited device coverage. Multiplayer is local. Touch controls are limited. Read the known limitations and privacy policy. The game is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any animation studio or rights holder.
