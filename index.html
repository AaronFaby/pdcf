Hold guard to soften hits. Kicks reach farther than punches. Landing and blocking hits builds ink; a signature move costs 35 ink. Popeye’s spinach restores a little health and boosts his punches for six seconds.

NEW TRICKS: P1 uses R to throw and left Shift to dash; P2 uses I and right Shift. Throws beat guard at close range. Punches and kicks work in the air. Connect a punch, then press kick or special to cancel into a combo. Mickey strikes quickly; Popeye trades speed for heavier blows.

CONTROLLERS: Stick / D-pad moves, A / ✕ jumps, X / □ punches, Y / △ kicks, B / ○ uses a special, LB / L1 guards, RB / R1 throws, RT / R2 dashes, and Start pauses. In menus A confirms and B goes back. Release buttons after changing screens.

TRAINING: Select Training before starting. Configure the dummy, recover health, refill ink, and inspect your inputs and combo damage. Backspace or controller View / Select resets practice instantly.
