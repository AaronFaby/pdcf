Privacy policy.

Effective and last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers Public Domain Cartoon Fighters at pdcf.dev and its save-recovery pages at pdcf.aaronfaby.com and www.pdcf.dev, including its game and information pages. “We” and “the maintainer” mean the independent project maintainer identified by the AaronFaby/pdcf repository.

The short version

You can play without an account. Saved progress and control, audio, and visual preferences stay in your browser. The game does not upload your button presses, matches, or tutorial progress. Cloudflare delivers the site, including its locally hosted fonts. Cloudflare browser analytics/performance metrics are intentionally kept enabled; their separate network processing is explained below. No automatic Google Fonts requests are made by this version.

1. Information the game uses

The current game has no player registration, login, payment form, online multiplayer, chat, or player-upload feature. It does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, precise location, or payment details.

Keyboard, touch, and controller inputs are processed on your device to run the game. Training displays recent inputs and combat results locally. Controller identifiers, button/axis state, and player assignments are read through your browser’s Gamepad API to support controls and reconnects; the game does not transmit these to the maintainer. Audio playback uses output sound, not microphone recording. The game does not request microphone, camera, or location access.

Temporary match state, Training feedback, guided-lesson progress, and controller assignments remain in the page’s memory and are discarded when the page closes or reloads. Your browser’s reduced-motion preference is used when motion is set to Follow system. Fullscreen is requested only from a player action and is not restored automatically on reload.

2. Saved browser data

The game uses local storage, a browser feature separate from cookies. These entries are stored for this site in the browser profile you use:

Game data saved on your device
EntryWhat it remembers
pdcf-arcade-v1Your selected fighter, difficulty, ladder, cleared bouts, attempts, losses, and run status, including an unlocked ending. Starting a new run replaces the previous checkpoint.
pdcf-controls-v1P1/P2 keyboard bindings, controller action bindings, and horizontal/vertical stick dead zones. It does not save your controller’s device identifier or a history of key presses.
pdcf-audio-v1Sound enabled/muted and music/effects volume preferences.
pdcf-visuals-v1Your motion mode (follow system, reduced, or full), screen shake, film effects, and moving-scenery preferences. Fullscreen state is not saved.

These entries are not sent to a game server or synced by the game across devices. People using the same browser profile may see or replace the same saves. Browser or device backup/sync features, if enabled separately, are controlled by your browser or device provider.

Saved entries have no automatic expiry in the game. They remain until replaced, cleared by you, or removed by your browser. Blocking storage leaves play and settings available for the current page session, but saved progress and preferences may not survive a reload.

Moving saves between addresses

Saved data belongs to each browser profile and site address. Redirecting to pdcf.dev does not transfer an old address’s saves. The save-transfer page can download a JSON backup of the four entries listed above, or import a file you choose after validation and confirmation of replacement. Files are processed locally in your browser; this tool does not upload their contents or put saved data in URLs. Entries not included in a backup are kept. Exporting does not delete or change the source entries, and importing does not delete the old address’s data. Close other game tabs before importing so old in-memory values cannot overwrite the imported data. Backups remain on your device until you delete them; treat them as private files. Normal Cloudflare page/performance metrics still apply to visits to this page.

3. Cookies, analytics & advertising

The game code does not set cookies, include advertising, install advertising pixels, or send gameplay analytics, session recordings, or behavioral profiles to the maintainer. Local storage is used for the player features listed above, not for advertising.

This does not mean that visiting the site creates no technical records. Cloudflare processes requests to deliver and protect the site and may use security cookies or similar mechanisms where its services require them. Hosting-level traffic/security statistics are distinct from the game’s local input history. Your browser may also cache game assets and font files.

The game does not implement a separate response to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals because its code does not perform advertising tracking, sell personal information, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Browser controls and provider-specific choices still apply to network services.

4. Hosting, fonts & external links

Cloudflare hosting and security

The site is delivered through Cloudflare Workers and its network. Requests necessarily expose information such as your IP address, requested URL, time, browser/request headers, and routing/security information to Cloudflare. These are used for delivery, reliability, and protection against abuse. Cloudflare may derive traffic statistics from requests. Its handling of this information is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Cloudflare browser metrics

The maintainer has chosen to keep Cloudflare Web Analytics/RUM enabled for page/performance measurements. A live browser check on October 18, 2026 observed a Cloudflare Web Analytics/RUM beacon loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com. This is hosting-provided script behavior; the repository’s information-page HTML does not embed it. The deployed site can therefore send browser page/performance measurements to Cloudflare automatically, separately from the game’s local combat state and manual feedback.

Cloudflare’s RUM documentation describes page/referrer information, a per-page identifier, and load/render/interaction timings. It states that the beacon does not read or write browser storage such as cookies or local storage. Its collection documentation identifies /cdn-cgi/rum as a reporting destination. The site’s complete dashboard configuration, regional behavior and provider retention have not been independently audited; this notice does not certify legal compliance or promise identical behavior for every visit.

Locally hosted fonts

Barlow Condensed and DM Sans are bundled with the game and downloaded from this site's assets/fonts/ directory through the same host as the other assets, including on these information pages. Loading them does not contact Google Fonts or another font provider. The maintainer obtained these files from the Google Fonts distribution; the included Barlow license and DM Sans license describe their SIL Open Font License terms. System fonts remain available if the files cannot load. Earlier versions requested fonts directly from Google; this change does not delete records those providers may already hold.

GitHub and other linked sites

The source, issue tracker, and reference materials are external links. Following them takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices. The GitHub icon is an inline image, not an embedded GitHub tracking widget. Game-footer links open in a new tab so you can keep your game open. If you contact the maintainer through an issue, GitHub handles the submission under GitHub’s privacy statement; your username and posted content can be public.

5. Purposes & applicable legal bases

Information is used to provide the game you request, remember optional preferences and progress, serve fonts/assets, keep the site available and secure, and respond to inquiries you choose to send. It is not used by the game to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, service delivery and requested features may be necessary to provide the requested service; reasonable security and reliability processing may rely on legitimate interests, subject to your rights. If an applicable law requires consent for a particular nonessential feature, that requirement applies; this policy itself is not a consent request and playing does not waive your privacy rights. Providers describe their own processing bases in their policies.

6. Disclosure & international processing

The maintainer does not sell personal information or share it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising. Network services described above receive data as part of serving the site. An inquiry you send is disclosed to the service you choose to send it through. Information held by the maintainer may also be disclosed where legally required or reasonably necessary to address abuse, security incidents, or legal claims.

Cloudflare and GitHub operate internationally, so network or inquiry data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Their policies describe applicable transfer arrangements. The game itself does not upload local saves for international storage.

7. Retention & security

Local save retention is controlled by your browser and the actions described in section 2. Temporary gameplay state ends with the page session. The maintainer does not run a separate player-account or gameplay-history database.

Hosting/security records follow the applicable Cloudflare service configuration and policies; this notice does not promise a fixed deletion period for provider records. Public GitHub issues can remain in the repository’s history and in third-party copies. Maintainer-held correspondence, if any, is kept as needed to address the inquiry and any applicable legal obligations.

The hosted site uses HTTPS. Browser storage is not an encrypted personal vault: anyone with access to the same browser profile, device, or suitable browser extensions may be able to access it. No website can guarantee absolute security. Do not include passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information in issues or game data.

8. Your choices & privacy rights

  • Delete saves: use your browser’s site-data settings to clear data for pdcf.dev and, separately, any previous address where you played (such as pdcf.aaronfaby.com or www.pdcf.dev). This removes the Arcade checkpoint and control, audio, and visual preferences. Reload or close open game tabs afterward so old in-memory settings are not saved again.
  • Reset controls: Options → Controls → Restore both players’ defaults changes the control profiles. It does not erase your Arcade run, audio preferences, or visual preferences.
  • Limit persistence: block site storage or use a private browser session. Your browser decides what is removed and when.
  • Limit external requests: browser privacy/network controls can block the Cloudflare metrics beacon; the game does not depend on it. Fonts are served by this site with system-font fallbacks. Blocking the game’s hosting requests prevents it from loading.
  • Control sound and motion: use Options → Picture & motion for saved visual settings, or choose Follow system to use your browser/device’s reduced-motion preference. Sound options control audio separately.

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on it; and complain to a relevant privacy authority. These rights have legal conditions and exceptions. Contact the maintainer using the method below to raise a request; do not post sensitive verification documents publicly.

The maintainer cannot remotely read or delete a save that exists only in your browser. Requests about data held independently by Cloudflare or GitHub, or by font providers used in earlier versions, can also be directed through those providers’ privacy channels.

9. Children & families

The game does not ask players for their age or other identifying details and has no player chat, registration, or server file-upload feature. Those choices do not eliminate the hosting and provider processing described above or establish compliance with children's privacy laws. An independent child-privacy review remains open. Cartoon presentation is not an assurance that every external site is suitable for children. Parents and guardians should supervise external links and public issue submissions. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the project, contact the maintainer without posting the child’s details publicly.
